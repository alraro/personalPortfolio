---
title: Left Joiners
subtitle: An accessible web portal with a statistical analysis of mental-health data.
---

Five of us built it in about five hours at the second Malackathon at the University of Málaga, from a medical dataset provided by Oracle. We won the NTT DATA prize for excellence in accessibility and usability.

The portal is built with Astro and its content collections, with the focus on making the data easy for anyone to read.

My part was cleaning and analysing the data with Python and pandas: date conversion, handling missing values and the derived columns the study relies on.
