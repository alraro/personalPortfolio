# Personal Portfolio

Alfonso Ramos Rojas's public portfolio site, aimed at recruiters: who he is, what he has built, and where he has worked and studied.

## Language

**Owner**:
The person the portfolio presents: Alfonso Ramos Rojas.
_Avoid_: User, author, me

**Visitor**:
Anyone browsing the portfolio; the primary target is a recruiter.
_Avoid_: User, reader

**Project**:
A piece of work the Owner built (personal, academic, 42 or at an Event) and showcases with media, a description and a link. A Project has its own name, which need not match its repository's name.
_Avoid_: Work, item, repo

**Featured Project**:
A Project the Owner has chosen to give prominent, large-format placement; every other Project is shown in a compact form.
_Avoid_: Highlighted, pinned, top project

**Media**:
The visual that represents a Project: an image or a short silent looping video, always with a still poster.
_Avoid_: Thumbnail, GIF, preview

**Primary Link**:
The single destination a Visitor reaches by clicking a Project: its own website if it has one, otherwise its repository.
_Avoid_: Main URL, href

**Experience**:
A role the Owner has held for an organization, such as a job or a research position.
_Avoid_: Job, employment, work

**Education**:
A formal or structured learning programme the Owner is enrolled in or completed, such as the university degree or 42.
_Avoid_: Studies, training

**Event**:
A hackathon, competition or volunteering occasion the Owner took part in, with the role they played. Talks attended are not Events.
_Avoid_: Activity, talk, conference

**Award**:
The placement or prize the Owner earned at an Event, such as "2nd place". A Project built at an Event inherits that Event's Award.
_Avoid_: Achievement, trophy, badge
