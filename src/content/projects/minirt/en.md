---
title: miniRT
subtitle: A ray tracer written from scratch in C.
---

A ray tracing engine in C that renders 3D scenes described in `.rt` files. It casts rays from a camera through each pixel, finds intersections with spheres, planes and cylinders, and shades them with ambient and diffuse light and hard shadows. The bonus version adds multithreaded tile rendering, several coloured lights, Phong specular reflection, checker patterns, texture and normal maps, and extra shapes. Built for the 42 curriculum, together with a teammate.
